JWE#3130 Inconel Wire Inserted Expanded Graphite Packing

Reinforced for extreme valve service

PACKING

Braided into a square cross-section from expanded graphite yarns reinforced with internal Inconel wire, engineered for extreme high-temperature and high-pressure valve applications. The integral metallic wire provides exceptional structural reinforcement and anti-extrusion resistance while retaining flexibility. Superior heat resistance, high thermal conductivity and self-lubrication ensure long service life under harsh conditions.
